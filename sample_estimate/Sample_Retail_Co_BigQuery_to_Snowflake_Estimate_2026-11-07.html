Assumptions and caveats

  • 16 BigQuery slots = 1 X-Small Snowflake node is a hardware comparison, not an exact performance match. Actual throughput varies by workload (ETL, BI, ad hoc).
  • The observed-period total uses the same per-project, per-month calculation as the Google Slot Calculator. The 3-year projection starts from that total's monthly average over 7 months: every calendar month from the first to the last month in the data (the app uses the same rule; a month with no rows counts as zero). Short periods, one-time loads, or seasonal peaks outside the window make the projection less reliable.
  • Active hours are each project's own measured wall-clock time with at least one query running (overlapping queries merged, never averaged across projects). Nodes are fractional and are not rounded up. Months where a project ran no queries are not included.
  • Snowflake does not charge for bytes scanned: compute is billed in credits only while a warehouse runs, plus storage.
  • Prices are list-price defaults unless marked as customer values in Inputs.
  • The 80% / 120% range reflects estimation uncertainty and should be widened if the source data is incomplete.
